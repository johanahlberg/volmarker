      Relevant events

      Only events on this list get a weight. An event matches on its Bloomberg ticker, or on its name (outside the home country with the country code in front, e.g. "GE IFO Business Climate"). It weighs on its option expiry: the first good day whose cut (the pair's Expiry cut in Setup, e.g. 10:00 New York) is after the release, so FOMC at 14:00 New York weighs on the next day. Several events on one expiry add up; the biggest names the day. A weight typed on a pair, or calibrated to the dailies, replaces the default for that date; 0 switches it off. One-off events (elections) can be typed on the pair. Saving applies to every pair straight away.
